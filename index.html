I am a political sociologist mainly concerned with studying power and social networks. Using computational methods, I examine how political and economic elites coordinate, how institutions gain or lose public trust, and how networks of money and organizations shape social and political outcomes. In my spare time, I enjoy spending time with my cat, composing atmospheric synth music, and playing Mahjong.
