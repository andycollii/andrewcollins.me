Sound, and other things

Outside of research, I compose atmospheric synth music and build my own audio effect plugins.

These are Audio Unit (AU) plugins for Logic Pro and other AU-compatible digital audio workstations. Both are open source on GitHub.

  • 2024
    I AM EXOTIC

    A piece of ambient music, animated and interpreted by visual artist Shaowen Liang.

    Watch on YouTube
  • AU
    PolyGate Sequencer

    A 16-step polyrhythmic gate sequencer that rhythmically ducks and mutes incoming audio, with adjustable time signatures, envelope controls, and per-step modulation.

    GitHub
  • AU
    PolyDucker

    A dual-module polyrhythmic ducking effect.

    GitHub
